# Chocolate Sales Data Analysis
# Data Source

The dataset used in this project was obtained from Kaggle – Chocolate Sales Dataset.

# 1. Project Objective

The objective of this analysis was to explore global chocolate sales trends and understand customer purchasing behavior across international markets. The study focused on identifying best-selling products, analyzing regional sales patterns, and examining the relationship between product price and sales volume.

# 2. Methodology

An exploratory data analysis (EDA) was conducted using international chocolate sales data. The process included:

Data cleaning and preprocessing
Trend and correlation analysis
Data visualization

The analysis aimed to identify sales patterns, product performance, seasonal trends, and pricing behavior to support business decision-making.

# 3. Tools and Technologies

The project was completed using:

Python
Visual Studio Code
Plotly Express
pandas
NumPy

# 4. Key Findings
Revenue by Country

Revenue contribution was relatively balanced across countries, with most markets contributing between 15–18% of total sales revenue. Australia generated the highest revenue, while New Zealand contributed the least.

Although overall revenue levels were similar, sales behavior varied between markets.

# Sales Trends and Seasonality

Global sales declined during the first months of the year before increasing significantly and peaking in June.

Different seasonal patterns were observed across countries:

New Zealand and the UK showed sharp sales spikes, likely linked to large or seasonal purchases.
India and Australia displayed steadier sales growth over time.
Product Performance

The top five products generated more than 26% of total revenue:

Smooth Silky Salty
50% Dark Bites
White Choc
Peanut Butter Cubes
Eclairs

# Customer preferences differed by region:

50% Dark Bites performed especially well in Australia.
Smooth Silky Salty and Eclairs were highly popular in India, Canada, and the UK.

The analysis also showed that high revenue did not always mean high sales volume. For example, White Choc generated strong revenue despite lower unit sales, suggesting higher profitability per product.

# Price Sensitivity

A negative correlation was found between price and sales volume across all countries, indicating that chocolate sales are generally price-sensitive.

However, the level of sensitivity varied:

Australia, Canada, and the USA showed the strongest response to price changes.
India showed more stable demand regardless of pricing fluctuations.

These results suggest that pricing strategies should be adapted to each market.
