# Chocolate-Sales-Data-Analysis

# Data Source

The dataset used in this project was obtained from Kaggle: Chocolate Sales Dataset: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/atharvasoundankar/chocolate-sales 

# 1. Project Objective

The purpose of this analysis was to examine global chocolate sales patterns and better understand customer purchasing behavior across international markets. The study focused on identifying the highest-performing chocolate products, evaluating regional sales trends, and exploring the relationship between product pricing and sales volume.

# 2. Methodology

An exploratory market analysis was performed using publicly available chocolate sales data. The process involved several stages, including data cleaning, preprocessing, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and data visualization. Patterns in customer demand, product performance, seasonal fluctuations, and pricing behavior were investigated to generate business-oriented insights that could support strategic decision-making.

# 3. Tools and Technologies

The analysis was completed using:

Python
Visual Studio Code
Plotly Express

Additional Python libraries such as pandas and NumPy were used for data manipulation and preprocessing.

# 4. Key Findings
# Revenue Distribution Across Countries

The contribution to total revenue was relatively balanced among the countries analyzed, with most markets accounting for approximately 15–18% of global sales. Australia generated the highest share of revenue, whereas New Zealand recorded the lowest contribution. Despite similar overall revenue shares, each market displayed distinct purchasing behaviors and sales dynamics.

# Sales Trends and Seasonal Patterns

Global chocolate sales showed a gradual decrease during the early months of the year before rising significantly and reaching their highest point in June.

# Different seasonal patterns were also observed between countries:

Markets such as New Zealand and the United Kingdom experienced sudden spikes in sales, suggesting the influence of bulk or seasonal purchases.
India and Australia demonstrated more stable and consistent sales growth over time.

These variations indicate that customer demand is influenced by regional market conditions and purchasing habits.

# Product Performance Analysis

A small group of products accounted for a significant proportion of overall revenue. The five leading products together generated more than one-quarter of total global sales revenue. These products included:

Smooth Silky Salty
50% Dark Bites
White Choc
Peanut Butter Cubes
Eclairs

# Consumer preferences differed across regions:

50% Dark Bites performed particularly well in Australia.
Smooth Silky Salty and Eclairs were among the strongest-performing products in India, Canada, and the United Kingdom.

The analysis also revealed that products generating the highest revenue were not always those sold in the largest quantities. For example, White Choc produced strong revenue despite comparatively lower sales volume, suggesting higher unit profitability or premium pricing.

# Price Sensitivity and Demand

The correlation analysis identified a negative relationship between product price and sales volume in all countries examined. This suggests that chocolate demand tends to decrease as prices increase.

However, the degree of price sensitivity varied by market:

Australia, Canada, and the United States showed the strongest negative correlation, indicating that consumers in these markets are more responsive to price changes.
India displayed weaker price sensitivity, suggesting that demand remained relatively stable even when prices fluctuated.

These findings may help businesses develop region-specific pricing and marketing strategies.

# Product Performance Analysis by Country

A deeper product-level analysis could be conducted to better understand how chocolate products perform in individual markets. This analysis may include:

Monitoring revenue and sales volume for both high-performing and underperforming products
Identifying products with growing or declining demand
Evaluating seasonal purchasing patterns and sales peaks for each product category

Additional techniques such as cumulative revenue analysis or rolling averages could be applied to reduce short-term fluctuations and reveal long-term trends more clearly.

# Monthly product performance could also be visualized using:

Line charts for trend analysis
Heatmaps for comparing product demand across countries and seasons

This would help businesses make more informed decisions regarding inventory management and promotional planning.

# Customer Segmentation and Purchasing Behavior

Further analysis could focus on customer segmentation by grouping transactions according to:

Purchase size
Product type

Regional market characteristics

This approach would help identify customer preferences and purchasing behavior in different countries. It could also support the discovery of:

Frequently purchased product combinations
High-value customer segments
Markets with the strongest growth potential

These insights may improve the effectiveness of targeted marketing campaigns and product recommendations.

An additional area for investigation is sales team performance and outlier detection. This could include:

Identifying sales representatives with exceptionally high or low revenue performance
Comparing sales activity across regions
Tracking the number of monthly transactions and active sellers

Analyzing the strategies used by top-performing sales representatives may help improve operational efficiency and sales performance across the organization.

# Sales Forecasting

Potential forecasting objectives include:

Predicting monthly revenue trends
Identifying periods of expected high demand
Supporting inventory and supply chain planning

Forecasting accuracy could be improved by incorporating external variables such as:

Public holidays
Promotional campaigns

Seasonal shopping behavior

These additions would provide a more realistic understanding of market demand fluctuations.

# Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, several practical business actions could be explored:

Schedule promotions during periods of lower demand
Prioritize inventory for consistently high-performing products
Adjust pricing strategies in markets with strong price sensitivity

The findings also suggest that pricing strategies should be adapted to local market behavior, particularly in countries where customer demand is highly responsive to price changes.
